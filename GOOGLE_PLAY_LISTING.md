## Full Description (4000 characters max)

📚 **Stay Organized, Stay Ahead**

StudyFlow is the ultimate assignment tracker designed for students who want to stay organized without the complexity. With intelligent priority sorting, beautiful glassmorphic design, and powerful features, managing your academic workload has never been easier or more enjoyable.

✨ **KEY FEATURES**

🎯 **Smart Priority System**
• High Priority: Assignments due within 4 days
• Coming Up: Assignments due within 1.5 weeks  
• Worry About Later: Assignments due in 3+ weeks
• Never miss a deadline with automatic categorization

📅 **Calendar Integration**
• Visual monthly calendar view
• See all assignments at a glance
• Easy date navigation

📊 **Progress Tracking**
• Monitor completion rates
• Track daily streaks
• View active, completed, and overdue tasks
• Motivating statistics dashboard

🎨 **Gorgeous Customization**
• Multiple gradient backgrounds
• Pattern overlays (dots, lines, waves, grid)
• Upload custom images or videos
• Solid color themes
• Glassmorphism effects (toggle on/off)

✏️ **Easy Assignment Management**
• Quick creation with all details
• Color-coded subjects
• Priority levels (High, Medium, Low)
• Edit and delete anytime
• One-tap completion

🔍 **Powerful Filtering**
• Search by title or description
• Filter by status (pending/completed)
• Filter by priority level
• Filter by subject

📱 **Mobile Optimized**
• Touch-friendly 44px tap targets
• Smooth 60fps animations
• Works on phones, tablets, and desktops
• Responsive design for all screen sizes
• Safe area support for notched devices

🔒 **Privacy First**
• 100% local storage - no servers
• No user accounts required
• No data collection or tracking
• No ads, ever
• Your data stays on your device

⚡ **Performance**
• Lightweight and fast
• Offline capable (PWA)
• Battery-efficient animations
• Performance mode for older devices

🌙 **Modern UI**
• Beautiful glassmorphism design
• Dark mode support
• Clean, intuitive interface
• Minimalist aesthetic

📚 **Perfect For**
• High school students
• College students
• Online learners
• Anyone with assignments to track

💡 **Why StudyFlow?**

Unlike complicated assignment trackers with features you'll never use, StudyFlow focuses on what matters: keeping you organized with a beautiful, distraction-free interface. The smart priority system automatically organizes your tasks so you always know what to tackle first.

No subscriptions. No ads. No data mining. Just a powerful tool to help you succeed.

🚀 **Get Started in Seconds**
1. Add your first assignment
2. Watch as StudyFlow organizes it by priority
3. Check off completed tasks
4. Track your progress

Join thousands of students who stay organized with StudyFlow!
