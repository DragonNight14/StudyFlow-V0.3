PRIVACY POLICY for StudyFlow

Last Updated: October 22, 2025

## Introduction

StudyFlow ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our assignment tracking application.

## Information We Collect

**WE DO NOT COLLECT ANY PERSONAL INFORMATION.**

StudyFlow operates entirely on your device using local storage. All your data including:
- Assignment information (titles, descriptions, due dates)
- Subject and class information
- Settings and preferences
- Background customizations
- Statistics and progress tracking

...is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage technology.

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- No cloud synchronization or backup services
- No user accounts or authentication required
- Data remains on your device until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app

## Data Collection

We DO NOT collect:
- Personal identification information
- Email addresses or phone numbers
- Location data
- Usage analytics or statistics
- Device information
- IP addresses
- Cookies (except for essential localStorage)
- Any form of tracking data

## Third-Party Services

StudyFlow does not integrate with or share data with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

## Data Security

Your data security is inherent to the app's design:
- All data stays on your device
- No network transmission of personal data
- No external servers or databases
- Protected by your device's security measures

## Children's Privacy

StudyFlow is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13, because:
- No personal information is collected
- No user accounts are required
- No communication features exist
- All data remains local to the device

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:
- **Access**: All your data is accessible through the app interface
- **Delete**: Clear data by clearing browser storage or uninstalling the app
- **Export**: Data can be exported through browser developer tools (localStorage)
- **Modify**: All data can be edited within the app

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.

## Data Retention

Data is retained indefinitely on your device until:
- You manually delete assignments or settings
- You clear your browser's localStorage
- You uninstall the application
- You clear site data for the StudyFlow domain

## International Users

Since all data is stored locally on your device, no international data transfer occurs. The app can be used anywhere in the world without privacy concerns related to data jurisdiction.

## Technical Implementation

StudyFlow uses:
- **localStorage**: Browser-based local storage for all data
- **Service Workers**: For offline functionality only (no data collection)
- **No Cookies**: Except essential localStorage which is required for the app to function

## Permissions (Android APK)

If using the Android APK version, the app may request:
- **Storage Permission**: Only to save and load custom backgrounds you choose to upload
- **No other permissions are required or requested**

## Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please visit the GitHub repository linked in the app.

## Consent

By using StudyFlow, you consent to this Privacy Policy. Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no data to consent to processing.

## Data Breach Notification

Since no data is collected or stored on external servers, there is no risk of data breach from our end. Your data security depends on your device's security.

## Compliance

This privacy policy is designed to comply with:
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- Other international privacy laws

Since no personal data is collected, transmitted, or processed externally, the app inherently complies with these regulations.

---

**Summary**: StudyFlow collects NO personal information. All data stays on your device. Your privacy is 100% protected.
